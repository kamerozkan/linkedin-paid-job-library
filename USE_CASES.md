# Research workflows and limits

These are candidate evaluation workflows. They do not establish customer demand, approved research scope or permission for a commercial product. Use synthetic fixtures until access and permitted use are verified.

## Payer and company interpretation

Check whether a normalized record preserves the company and payer separately, together with its source identifier and observation time. Do not treat either string as a verified legal entity. A future permitted research project could compare those fields after establishing the meaning and coverage of the source response.

## Country and impression analysis

Test that estimated ranges keep their original bounds and do not acquire invented endpoint inclusivity. Keep country source keys and raw values available for inspection. A range or rounded percentage cannot support precise spend or unique-audience estimates.

## Source quality assessment

Check that a denied source request produces a diagnostic and no fabricated dataset rows. Compare useful row counts with the run summary. A short page, cap or missing pagination metadata is a coverage limitation, not evidence that the archive is exhausted.

## Dated metadata comparison

A digest may help compare two observations of the same identified record. The candidate does not implement a production monitor or infer job closure. Any future comparison requires an explicit query scope, dated source provenance and verified scan completeness.

## First live validation, if permitted

Use one narrow query and small limits after approved access and permission are confirmed. Inspect the actual response, normalized row quality, dataset counts, summary and cost. Do not enable a schedule or make reliability claims based solely on offline tests or platform success.
